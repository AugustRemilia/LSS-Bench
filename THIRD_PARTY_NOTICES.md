# Third-Party Notices

## MathDial

The initial mathematics material in LSS-Bench was developed from MathDial:

> Jakub Macina, Nico Daheim, Sankalan Pal Chowdhury, Tanmay Sinha, Manu Kapur,
> Iryna Gurevych, and Mrinmaya Sachan. "MathDial: A Dialogue Tutoring Dataset
> with Rich Pedagogical Properties Grounded in Math Reasoning Problems."
> Findings of EMNLP, 2023.

Project: <https://github.com/eth-nlped/mathdial>
